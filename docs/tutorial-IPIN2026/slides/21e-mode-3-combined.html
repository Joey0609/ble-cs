Mode 3: combine timing and phase in one step

T_IP2 separates the two directions. Timing and phase share a step but need separate quality checks.

Walk through the exact conceptual order used in the planner: initiator synchronization packet followed by its tones, turnaround, reflector tones followed by its synchronization packet. Guard periods are not drawn here. Mode three is not a promise that adding two estimators produces perfect ranging, and the host does not automatically implement a robust fused estimator simply because both traces are available. It provides a convenient way to examine timing and phase evidence from related exchanges, at a different airtime cost from tone-only steps.
