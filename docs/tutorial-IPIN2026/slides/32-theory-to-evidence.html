In the Configuration view, press Open to browse the examples presented directly by the file dialog and load a configuration. Before applying it, walk through the generated schedule: the connection timeline, procedures, events and subevents, individual steps, and channel sequence. Relate each component to the loaded settings and distinguish the illustrative controller values from the requested configuration.

Apply the configuration, then press Scan to discover peers. Inspect the found devices' names and Bluetooth addresses. Match each discovered Tag or Reflector to its assigned ID using the appropriate table, then select your assigned peer before connecting. Tags advertise as CSTag followed by their address without colons. The other reflectors advertise as CSReflector followed by their address without colons. For example, CSTagEDE1EB098EEE matches ED:E1:EB:09:8E:EE, which is Tag 1 in the table. Scan results continue to arrive until participants explicitly connect to their selected peer or stop scanning. These addresses come from the workshop device screenshots. Then run a controlled pairwise ranging experiment and inspect the observations.
