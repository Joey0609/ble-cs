Introduce SensWear as a reusable physical AI platform: acquire signals from the physical world, interpret them locally or in an application, and use sensing or actuation to close a loop. The nRF54L15 is the Bluetooth-capable processor used for this tutorial's platform story. The IMU adds motion information and daughterboards make sensing and actuation configurable. Open source is the project model; the campaign page says public source publication is planned before launch, so avoid telling participants that every advertised repository is already published today.
