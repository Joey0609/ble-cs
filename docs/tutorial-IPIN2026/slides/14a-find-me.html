Find Me: presence, on request

SINCE
Bluetooth 4.0 LE, Find Me Profile (2011)
LINK
A connection: the phone connects to the tag
TRIGGER
The user taps “find”; the phone writes to the tag’s Immediate Alert Service
RESULT
The tag beeps; the person walks to the sound
LIMITATIONS
  • No range, direction or position: presence only
  • The person does the localization, by ear
  • Only on request, and only within connection range
  • No continuous estimate, so no track or record
Bluetooth SIG · Find Me Profile 1.0|Immediate Alert Service 1.0

Find Me is the first Bluetooth LE answer to “where is it?”, and it does not measure anything. The phone, in the Find Me Locator role, connects to the tag, the Find Me Target, when the user asks, and writes an Alert Level to the tag’s Immediate Alert Service. The tag beeps or blinks; the person does the localization by ear. There is no range, direction or position, it works only within connection range and only while someone is looking, and nothing is recorded. Do not confuse it with the crowd-sourced Find My and Find Hub networks: there, other people’s phones hear a tag’s advertisements and report their own GPS position. Keep one point for later: Find Me already runs over a connection, and so does Channel Sounding.
