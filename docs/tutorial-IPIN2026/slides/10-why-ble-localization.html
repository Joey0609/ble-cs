Why localize with Bluetooth Low Energy (LE)?

01 · ALREADY CARRIED

Phones + wearables

The devices people already carry can take part, with no extra radio.

02 · LOW POWER

Small batteries

Tags, badges and rings can range on the same radio that sends their data.

03 · STANDARD

One specification

Since Bluetooth 6.0, two devices from any vendor can measure distance and check it.

Location becomes a feature of a link you already have, not a separate system.
Bluetooth SIG · technical overview

Once location is worth having, ask why Bluetooth LE, when UWB, Wi-Fi and cameras exist. The answer is reach and cost: the radio is already in phones, wearables and tags, it is designed for small batteries, and the ranging runs over the same connection that carries the data. Channel Sounding, added in Bluetooth 6.0, makes distance part of the specification, so devices from different vendors can measure it and check it against relay attacks. Avoid claiming that every phone supports Channel Sounding today, or that Bluetooth LE always beats UWB; controller and phone support are still arriving.
