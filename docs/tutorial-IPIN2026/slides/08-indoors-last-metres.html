The last few metres are often indoors

A useful answer is more than a dot: where, when, and how certain?

Start with a concrete scene: someone looking for shared lab equipment, a robot trying to rendezvous with a person, or a participant following an indoor route. The building creates a different propagation environment from open sky. We need a local coordinate frame, useful update rate and an uncertainty estimate. Invite a show of hands for people who have tried positioning inside a lab, hospital, warehouse or campus. Avoid claiming that every application needs centimetres or that one technology always wins. The floor plan is a conceptual illustration, not a measured deployment.
