ACL timing is the clock for Channel Sounding

CS procedures start at an offset from an ACL anchor and repeat in whole connection intervals

Connection interval× 1.25 ms7.5 ms
CS offsetµs after the anchorLL chooses
Subevent lengthµs6 ms
Procedure interval× connection interval1–4

Ranging rate = 1 / (procedure interval × connection interval): 2 × 7.5 ms → 67 Hz

Every LE connection has anchor points spaced by the connection interval, a multiple of 1.25 ms; 7.5 ms is the classic minimum and this project's default. In the ACL event at each anchor the Central transmits first and the Peripheral replies after T_IFS, 150 µs. Peripheral latency lets the Peripheral skip events without moving the anchors; the supervision timeout ends the link after that long without a valid packet. Channel Sounding is scheduled on this clock. LL_CS_REQ names the connection event where the procedure starts and an offset window from that event's anchor, in microseconds. Within a procedure, the CS event interval and the procedure interval are counted in ACL connection intervals, the subevent interval in 625 µs units, the subevent length in microseconds, and the maximum procedure length in 625 µs units. So the ACL interval quantizes everything above one subevent. The figure uses the firmware defaults from cs_config.c: 7.5 ms interval, 6 ms subevent budget, mode 2 with a mode-1 sub-mode and two mode-0 steps. The planner's model fits 14 steps into 5.96 ms. The firmware allows a procedure interval of 1 to 4 and the controller selects the value; 2 is drawn. The 1.5 ms offset and 1 ms of ACL airtime are the planner's illustrative assumptions, not measurements. The ACL events keep running during ranging. They carry the RAS notifications with the reflector's step data, so every returned result costs ACL airtime; the next slide sizes that traffic. The update rate follows directly: one distance estimate per procedure, every procedure interval times connection interval. At 2 × 7.5 ms that is 15 ms, about 67 Hz; at 4 × 7.5 ms it is 30 ms, about 33 Hz. A longer connection interval slows ranging even if the CS workload is unchanged.
