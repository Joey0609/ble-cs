Our CS app · source notes

Name the application: ble-channel-sounding, the Python/PyQt6 desktop host in the supplied ble-cs repository. Its purpose is to connect the theory to a visible experiment with configuration, controller feedback, raw data and replay. In the documented hosted setup, a native-USB nRF54LM20 DK runs cs_client and connects to a reflector. The controllers schedule and time the RF exchanges; the host PC only configures them and analyses what they report, so the app never times the radio. The Results view now includes an IFFT range profile of the complex PBR products and IFFT peak distances beside phase-slope and RTT estimates. Radio Test is disabled in this deployment. The simulator exercises the workflow without proving RF accuracy. Press down for the four parts of the app: planning, running, inspection and replay. Inspection takes three slides so participants can see controller feedback, PBR with IFFT and the estimate comparison. Press right to continue to the lab question.
