Our CS app · source notes

Describe what the repository actually implements. Pairing uses configuration ID, start ACL event and procedure counter; steps are paired in their common order and mismatched channels are discarded. The selected antenna path and quality filter affect both PBR estimates. The IFFT uses the corrected complex products on their 1 MHz channel grid; missing channels are left empty. Zero padding samples the profile more finely without improving physical resolution. RTT uses paired time differences, validity and packet-quality information; its mean and median summarize accepted steps. A low spread does not eliminate calibration bias. Check controller reports and missing or incomplete procedures. This is a transparent educational pipeline, not a claim that a simple fit or the largest IFFT peak identifies the first path in multipath.
