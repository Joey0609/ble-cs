Core 6.0 · Vol 6, Part B (Link Layer)

A connection starts from advertising. The advertiser sends connectable ADV_IND packets on the three primary advertising channels, 37, 38 and 39, once per advertising interval. The scanner listens; when it hears an ADV_IND it wants to connect to, it answers on the same channel right after the packet with CONNECT_IND. From that moment the scanner is the Central and the advertiser the Peripheral of one LE ACL connection. With extended advertising the same happens with AUX_ADV_IND, AUX_CONNECT_REQ and AUX_CONNECT_RSP on a secondary channel. Watch the words: the Link Layer calls the scanning side the initiator while it tries to connect, which has nothing to do with the CS initiator. CONNECT_IND fixes the link's timing and channels: connection interval, Peripheral latency, supervision timeout, channel map and hopping, plus the access address, CRC seed and a transmit window for the first packet. The timing can change later with a connection parameter update. Three things are not in CONNECT_IND; they are negotiated over the new link right after it forms. The PHY: the link starts on the PHY the connect request used, 1M with legacy advertising or the secondary advertising PHY with extended advertising, and a PHY update can move it to 2M or Coded. The Link Layer data length, the largest payload per packet. And the ATT MTU, which is a GATT exchange, not a Link Layer one.
