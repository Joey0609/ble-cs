Dariush Salami · Yusein Ali
IPIN 2026 · Theory & demo

IPIN 2026 · tutorial·SensWear platform

Open with the ring as an ordinary object that can become a measurement instrument. The question for this session is how radio observations become trustworthy information about motion indoors. This is the first 90 minutes of the conference's 180-minute tutorial; the second part is practical work. Introduce the tutors without relying on the inconsistent affiliation text on the conference page. Tell the audience that the goal is to understand, configure and critique the measurements, then use them in an indoor-positioning experiment.
