Preview the two measurement principles before deriving them. RTT cares about extremely small differences between timing intervals; PBR turns variation over frequency into delay. PBR is attractive for fine ranging, but phase ambiguity and multipath matter. RTT provides a complementary check and participates in security designs. The app's separate mean, median and slope traces make these distinctions visible; they are not a mandated Bluetooth fusion algorithm. Ask the audience which observable they expect to change when the reflector processing delay changes.
