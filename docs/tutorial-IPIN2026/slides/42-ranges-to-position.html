Several ranges become a position estimate

p̂ = arg minₚ Σᵢ wᵢ (‖p − aᵢ‖ − rᵢ)²

aᵢ: surveyed anchor position
rᵢ: calibrated range
wᵢ: justified measurement weight

In 2D: three non-collinear ranges can suffice ideally; four or more give useful redundancy.

This is the bridge to the second part, not a statement that the desktop app contains a multilateration tab. Define the weighted nonlinear least-squares objective. The weights may reflect uncertainty or validated quality information, but raw quality labels are not automatically inverse variances. For a floor solution, account for anchor and target height: a radio range is three-dimensional, not automatically a horizontal distance. Either model height in the residual or project using justified heights. Three non-collinear anchors give an ideal two-dimensional solution; extra well-placed anchors help diagnose inconsistency and maintain coverage when one link fails. Outliers may require robust losses or rejection before solving.
