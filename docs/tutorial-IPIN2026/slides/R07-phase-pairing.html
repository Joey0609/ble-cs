Use complex notation only as much as needed: z equals I plus jQ, and its angle is the measured phase. In the conventional two-way exchange, combining matched phase observations cancels the opposing initial oscillator phase terms under the model assumptions. The application's baseline computes the complex product, not the conjugate product. The minus sign follows its receive-phase convention; other conventions may flip the sign. Residual frequency error and hardware effects remain. Peer data are therefore needed for this baseline analysis. Inline PCT Transfer is a newer extension with a different data path and is kept out of this baseline derivation.
