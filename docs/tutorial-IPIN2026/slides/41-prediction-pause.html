Prediction pause: which explanation survives?

1. The reflector takes longer to respond. Does the true range grow?
No. Corrected RTT subtracts its turnaround interval.
2. The phase curve looks clean. Can the range still be biased?
Yes. Calibration error or a dominant reflected path can remain.
3. The planner changes its random seed. Did we set the radio’s hop sequence?
No. The preview uses an example PRNG, not the controller’s CS DRBG.

Allocate roughly forty seconds per question: brief individual prediction, a show of hands, then reveal the answer. Question one checks whether the timing subtraction is understood. Question two checks whether precision is being confused with accuracy. Question three prevents a particularly misleading interpretation of the planner. Keep this interactive even if the room is quiet by offering two possibilities and asking participants to explain their choice to a neighbour.
