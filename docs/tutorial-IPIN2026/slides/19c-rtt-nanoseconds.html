Our CS app · source notes

Interactive prediction: increase the turnaround while holding the distance fixed. The initiator's total elapsed time increases, but the corrected propagation time and range stay fixed. Then increase distance and observe the much smaller nanosecond change. At three metres, round-trip flight time is about 20.01 ns. A one-nanosecond error after subtraction creates about 0.15 m of range error. The application decodes time_difference in 0.5 ns units; quantization is not a promise of corresponding accuracy. Use exact c internally and rounded display values. This demonstration is a mathematical model, not captured hardware timing.
