Zoom in: procedure → event → subevent → step

Our CS app · source notes·Bluetooth Core 6.0 · Vol 6, Part B

Start with the smallest unit: a step is an RF exchange with one mode on a channel. A subevent groups steps within a reserved time budget. Events organize subevents, and a procedure groups events into a ranging operation. Repeated procedures supply measurements over time. These are CS events; distinguish them from the ACL connection events that anchor scheduling and carry control or data transfer. The app offers separate views for connection, procedures, events and subevents, individual steps and channels. This nesting explains why changing one small timing parameter can affect a much larger schedule.
