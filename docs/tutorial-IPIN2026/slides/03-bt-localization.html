A short history of location with Bluetooth Low Energy. Find Me (2011) only answers "is it in range?": the phone connects and makes the tag beep, and you find it by ear. Beacons such as Apple's iBeacon (2013) advertise an identifier and a calibrated transmit power; the receiver converts RSSI to a distance. RSSI varies with fading, orientation and the human body, so the result is zones and metres of error. Direction Finding (Bluetooth 5.1, 2019) adds a Constant Tone Extension that a receiver samples while switching across an antenna array. Phase differences between antennas give an angle of arrival or departure; a position needs several fixed locators. Channel Sounding (Bluetooth 6.0, 2024) measures distance directly between two devices, from phase across many frequencies and from round-trip time, with security features against relay attacks. The underlying quantity moves from signal strength to phase and time.
