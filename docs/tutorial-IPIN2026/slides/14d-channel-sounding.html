Bluetooth SIG · technical overview|Core 6.0 · Channel Sounding

Channel Sounding, added in Bluetooth 6.0, measures distance between two devices and combines the observables of the earlier techniques. Both ends transmit, over a connection as in Find Me. The round-trip time of packets gives a coarse distance with no ambiguity. The phase of tones exchanged on many channels, up to 72 of them 1 MHz apart, falls linearly with frequency; the slope gives a fine distance, and the two-way exchange cancels the unknown oscillator phases. The amplitude of each tone comes with the same measurement and shows signal quality and fading from multipath. The plot is a sketch, not a measurement. Keep the callout from later in mind: the radio supplies observations, the estimator supplies the distance.
