Five parts, each feeding the hands-on. SensWear is the platform the radio lives on. Localization and ranging places Channel Sounding among the four ways to get distance from a radio. The BLE connection part matters because CS has no link of its own: the connection interval, PHY, data length and MTU decide when CS runs and how fast results come back. The Channel Sounding part is the longest: what is measured, how exchanges are organized and what invalidates a result. The hands-on then changes one parameter at a time. Questions and prediction pauses are included in the timings. The appendix is optional and outside the 90-minute total.
