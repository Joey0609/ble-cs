Eight parts, each feeding the hands-on. SensWear is the platform the radio lives on. Localization and ranging places Channel Sounding among the four ways to get distance from a radio. The LE link part introduces CS and the connection it needs: CS has no link of its own, so the connection interval, PHY, data length and MTU decide when CS runs and how fast results come back. Measurement principles covers what CS observes: the phase of tones, the round-trip time of packets, and the frequency calibration both depend on. The CS procedure part organizes those measurements into steps, subevents, events and procedures, sets them up, and delivers the reflector's results. Security explains why a distance must be protected and how CS does it. In practice turns all of this into the choices an implementer makes. The hands-on shows how the app checks a range estimate, then changes one parameter at a time. Questions and prediction pauses are included in the timings. The appendix is optional and outside the 90-minute total.
