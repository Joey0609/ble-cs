Our CS app · source notes

Give a concrete two-minute demonstration or narrate the screenshot. In Configuration, change a mode or phase-measurement preference and watch the schedule. The app places values selected by the controller in a separate example panel; do not present those as commands sent to the device. Negotiated reports replace the illustrative values when a link exists. The preview also models report transfer over RAS, with the negotiated MTU relevant to its timing. Ask participants to predict the effect of adding an antenna path before changing the control. Use the local screenshot if hardware or the live demonstration is unavailable.
