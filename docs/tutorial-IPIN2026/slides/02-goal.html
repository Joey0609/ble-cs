Our CS app · source notes

State the goal before any theory, so every later section has a place to land. The kit is two nRF54 boards and a laptop. The nRF54LM20 DK runs cs_client and talks to our desktop app over native USB; it is the CS initiator and connects over Bluetooth to the reflector, an nRF54L15 DK or the two-antenna nRF54L15 Tag. The app sets the CS configuration, starts the session and shows the raw steps and the estimates. The screenshot is simulated data from the same app. Everything in the next sections explains one of the parameters we will change in the hands-on, and what that change should do to the result.
