RTT: remove the time spent inside the reflector

Read the four timestamps in order. The initiator measures t4 minus t1 on its clock. The reflector measures t3 minus t2 on its own clock. Subtracting the turnaround removes the time when a signal was not in flight. Divide the remaining round trip by two, then multiply by propagation speed. This model assumes reciprocal propagation and compatible calibrated time scales; it does not require comparing absolute clock epochs. Real implementations also address frequency offset, timestamp bias and packet quality. Host arrival timestamps are not substitutes for controller measurements.
