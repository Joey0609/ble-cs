Distance is the slope across frequency

Differentiate the ideal phase model with respect to frequency. The intercept drops out, leaving minus four pi times range divided by c. The app groups repeated observations by channel, averages complex products for a chosen antenna path, unwraps the phases and fits a line. Do not average wrapped angles arithmetically. Fitting across more well-spaced frequencies can help constrain the slope, but it does not guarantee removal of reflected paths. Explain that sign conventions must be consistent through data decoding, correction and range estimation; avoid silently taking an absolute value to hide a sign error.
