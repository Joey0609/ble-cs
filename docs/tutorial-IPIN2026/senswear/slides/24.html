Mode 1: timestamp the packet exchange

Connect this mode directly to the four-timestamp derivation. The CS_SYNC structure and RTT sequence choice affect what is transmitted and the airtime. The application pairs timing data from both devices, then applies validity and packet-quality criteria. Timestamp unit size is different from empirical range accuracy. In the practical session, begin by inspecting the RTT tab and the accepted-pair count before interpreting the mean or median.
