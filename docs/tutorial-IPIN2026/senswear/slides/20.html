Try it: move the target, watch the phase

Give the audience thirty seconds to predict the result of increasing distance. Move the slider from three to six metres; the unwrapped slope magnitude doubles and wrapped phase cycles faster. Toggle wrapped phase off to make the slope intuitive. Add the echo, which is a weaker path with four metres extra path length, and show curvature in the phase response and deviation of the simple slope estimate. Explain the assumptions used in this model: reciprocal static channel, no clock error, no measurement noise, and a complex two-way product. It illustrates sensitivity rather than predicting performance of the hardware.
