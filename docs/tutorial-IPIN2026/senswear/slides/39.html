Tracking needs time, and evaluation needs truth

A moving ring is not at the same point during every pairwise link. Attach timestamps and understand the age of each range before solving. Temporal filtering can reduce jitter but introduce delay and hide failures; compare filtered and unfiltered estimates. IMU measurements can extend the model, but inertial integration drifts and requires careful fusion. For evaluation, use labelled checkpoints or an independent reference trajectory. Report the median and a tail percentile such as the 95th, plus valid-fix availability and latency. Do not discard failed fixes from the story. Calibrate on separate data to avoid evaluating on what the estimator was tuned to.
