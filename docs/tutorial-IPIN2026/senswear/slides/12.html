Initiator and reflector are active participants

Define the roles in the CS step. Both devices transmit, receive and measure; an ordinary wall reflection is not the protocol reflector. The CS roles and the GAP connection roles are distinct concepts. This project's normal teaching arrangement uses a central initiator and peripheral reflector, but we should not turn that arrangement into a universal definition. At the service layer, the Ranging Requestor consumes ranging data and the Ranging Responder supplies it; those are another layer of terminology. A fixed anchor can be either CS role if the chosen stack and scheduling support it.
