From raw observations to a defensible range

Describe what the repository actually implements. Pairing uses configuration ID, start ACL event and procedure counter; steps are paired in their common order and mismatched channels are discarded. The selected antenna path and quality filter affect the PBR data used. RTT uses paired time differences, validity and packet-quality information; its mean and median summarize accepted steps. A low spread does not eliminate calibration bias. Check controller reports and missing or incomplete procedures. This is a transparent educational pipeline, not a claim that a simple fit has the multipath robustness of a production vendor estimator.
