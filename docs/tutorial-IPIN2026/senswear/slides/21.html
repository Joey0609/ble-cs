Tiny frequency errors can become range bias

The two tone observations do not occur at the same instant. Residual oscillator frequency difference rotates the combined phase during their separation. Since a fractional offset is proportional to RF frequency, the error can also tilt the fitted slope. Work through the numbers: c times 12e-6 times 69e-6 divided by two is about 0.124 m. The app explicitly says the reported-offset sign convention is not pinned down in its educational analysis and exposes a sign control. Do not claim the selected correction is universally correct. Distinguish measured offset, controller-reported compensation, and residual offset; avoid compensating twice.
