Follow the complete measurement chain

Walk through one pairwise experiment. The host requests a configuration that both controllers can support. The controller schedules time-critical RF exchanges; the host PC is not timing the radio. Results reach the application, including the peer's information where required. The analysis pairs corresponding reports and estimates a range. In this repository, configuration, wire protocol, results analysis and visualization are separate components. That separation is educational: an unexpected range can be traced backward to a configuration, a timing choice or the raw step observations.
