SensWear platform

One electronic platform. Many physical roles.

Explain the separation between electronics, modules and enclosure. A ring is the form factor used in the tutorial title, but does not define the entire platform. The same design idea supports a body-worn sensor, a module attached to an object, or a fixed node on a wall. Changing placement also changes the antenna environment; enclosure and body effects require measurement again. A module is not automatically a localization anchor: it becomes one when its position is surveyed and the appropriate firmware role is configured.
